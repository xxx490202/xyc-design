How it is cut

Four periods around one death.

  1. T1
    Before · controversy

    The public argument that was already running while the person was alive.

  2. T2
    The death · outbreak

    The window right after the announcement, when volume peaks and the tone flips.

  3. T3
    Mourning · sustained

    Condolence settles into ritual: recollections, quotes, the same clips again.

  4. T4
    Long memory

    Months later, what is left: anniversaries, references, an occasional return.
